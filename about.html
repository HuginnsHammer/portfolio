ABOUT / WORKING APPROACH

Learning security by doing the work.

I’m Edward Mwaura, a Bachelor of Science in Computer Security and Forensics student at Meru University of Science and Technology in Kenya. I enjoy building tools, testing ideas, and figuring out why a system behaves the way it does.

What I’m working toward

I’m working toward a career in cybersecurity, especially security engineering and AI application security. I learn by writing code, testing it, and going back to fix what doesn’t work.

What I’m building

  • CloudSentry, a local scanner for selected infrastructure and container configuration risks
  • LLMShield, a gateway prototype for checking AI application requests and responses

How I approach technical work

I like work that can be checked. I try to keep experiments reproducible, handle evidence carefully, and explain the difference between what I observed and what I think it means.

I’m still learning, and I try to be clear about what I have actually tested and what still needs work.
