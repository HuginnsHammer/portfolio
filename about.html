ABOUT / WORKING APPROACH

Learning security by doing the work.

I’m Edward Mwaura, based in Kenya and studying cybersecurity and digital forensics. I enjoy working through technical problems, building tools to understand them, and writing down what I find so other people can follow the reasoning.

What I’m working toward

My long-term goal is to work in security engineering, with a growing interest in AI security. For now, I’m focused on the fundamentals: writing code, testing ideas, understanding failure modes, and learning from the results.

Areas I’m exploring

  • Cybersecurity engineering and defensive tooling
  • Digital forensics and incident investigation
  • Cloud and infrastructure security
  • Detection engineering and security testing
  • AI security and adversarial machine learning

How I approach technical work

I like work that can be checked. I try to keep experiments reproducible, handle evidence carefully, and explain the difference between what I observed and what I think it means.

I’m early in my career, so I put the emphasis on the work itself: the problem, the approach, the result, and what I would improve next.
