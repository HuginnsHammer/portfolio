ABOUT / WORKING APPROACH

A security learner who values evidence.

I’m Edward Mugo, based in Kenya, studying cybersecurity and digital forensics. I’m building practical skills by investigating systems, writing small tools, and learning to communicate technical findings clearly.

What I’m working toward

I want to become a stronger security engineer and, over time, contribute to AI security engineering and research. That means learning the fundamentals, building and testing real things, understanding failure modes, and being candid about what I have not yet verified.

Areas I’m exploring

  • Cybersecurity engineering and defensive tooling
  • Digital forensics and incident investigation
  • Cloud and infrastructure security
  • Detection engineering and security testing
  • AI security and adversarial machine learning

How I approach technical work

I prefer clear scope, small reproducible experiments, careful handling of evidence, and documentation that makes it possible for another person to challenge or repeat a result. I try to distinguish direct observations from interpretation and to treat limitations as part of the result rather than fine print.

This site is a work in progress. Its purpose is to show real work as it develops, not to imply a level of experience that the evidence does not support.
