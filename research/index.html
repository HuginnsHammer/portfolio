QUESTIONS BEFORE CONCLUSIONS

Research starts with a testable question.

I’m especially interested in detection engineering, AI security, and infrastructure hardening. I use labs to test ideas and understand the trade-offs before drawing conclusions.

01 / DETECTION ENGINEERING

From signal to useful alert

Explore how detection logic can be evaluated against expected behavior, benign activity, and edge cases. Future write-ups should include the data source, assumptions, rule logic, test cases, and false-positive trade-offs.
